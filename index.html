Our story

Where heritage meets hospitality.

Rotlo began with a simple belief — real Gujarati food deserves to be celebrated. So we took the recipes our grandmothers swore by and gave them a home worthy of them: warm, beautiful spaces where every meal feels like an occasion.

Fresh masalas. Time-honoured recipes. Unlimited thalis served with genuine warmth. Whether you dine in, order delivery, or book us for an event — you taste the same care every single time.
